Privacy Policy

PrismScale Live Wall · Last updated 16 November 2026

PrismScale Live Wall ("the app", "we") is run by PrismScale Private Limited, India. It shows a moderated selection of public social media posts on screens at live events. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, how long we keep it, and how to have it removed.

1. What the app does

For a brand's event, our staff enter the event hashtag. The app looks up recent public Instagram posts that use that hashtag, through Meta's official Instagram Graph API. Each post goes into a private approval queue. A member of our staff approves or hides it. Only approved posts are shown on the event screens.

2. Information we use

  • Public Instagram posts that use the event hashtag: the post's media, caption, link, time posted and the public username, as provided by Meta. We do not collect followers, messages, emails or any private information.
  • Our own staff accounts: the name, email and account ID of the staff members who sign in to operate a wall, so only authorised people can approve or hide posts.
  • Connected Facebook Page and Instagram professional account: when our staff connect our own Page and Instagram account, Meta gives the app an access token. We use it only to run the hashtag search for active events. It is stored on the server, never in the event screens.

3. How we use it

  • To show approved posts on screens at the event they were posted for.
  • To let staff moderate (approve, hide, pin) posts before anything is shown.
  • To keep the screens running if the venue internet drops, using short-term copies of approved posts.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, build profiles of people, or give attendees' posts to the brand to download or reuse.

4. Who can see it

  • People at the event see approved posts on the screens.
  • Our staff see the approval queue.
  • Service providers that host the app for us: Google Firebase (database and storage), GitHub Pages (web pages) and Meta (the Instagram API). They process data only to provide those services.

5. How long we keep it

  • Event posts and any copies of their media are deleted within 7 days after the event ends.
  • A post that is hidden or reported is removed from the screens and deleted straight away.
  • Access tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected or the app is removed.
  • Staff account details are kept while that person operates walls for us.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Remove your post: if a post of yours appears on a wall, email us with the post link and we will remove it from the screens and delete our copy, usually within 24 hours and never later than 7 days. Deleting the post on Instagram, or making your account private, also stops it from appearing.
  • Delete your data: see our data deletion instructions.
  • You can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us.

7. Security

Only signed-in staff can moderate a wall. Access tokens stay on the server. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Children

The app is for brands and event organisers. It is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
