Data Deletion Instructions

PrismScale Live Wall · Last updated 16 November 2026

PrismScale Live Wall keeps very little data, and only for a short time. Event posts are deleted automatically within 7 days after each event. You can also ask us to delete your data at any time.

If your Instagram post appeared on one of our walls

  1. Email dharam@prismscale.com with the subject "Live Wall removal request".
  2. Include the link to your Instagram post (or your username and the event name).
  3. We remove the post from the screens and delete our copy, usually within 24 hours and never later than 7 days, and reply to confirm.

Deleting the post on Instagram, or making your account private, also stops it from appearing on any wall.

If you connected a Facebook Page or Instagram account to the app

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  2. Find PrismScale Live Wall and choose Remove.
  3. This stops the app's access immediately. We delete the stored access token and any data linked to it within 7 days. You can also email us to have it done straight away.

If you are one of our staff operators

Email us and we will delete your operator account and its sign-in details within 7 days.
